Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know to plan your next luxury stay with complete peace of mind.

Have Questions? We Are Here to Help

To ensure a smooth and transparent experience, we have gathered the most common questions from our guests. If you cannot find the answer you are looking for, feel free to contact us directly via WhatsApp.

Bookings & Payments
How can I make a reservation?

You can browse and book our properties by clicking on our Accommodations section. All our links will redirect you directly and securely to our official Airbnb profiles, ensuring a globally protected transaction.

What are the cancellation policies?

Cancellation policies vary depending on the selected property and travel dates. You can review the specific details regarding refund conditions before confirming your payment directly within the booking platform.

Check-In & Check-Out
What are the Check-in and Check-out times?

Standard entry time (Check-in) is from 3:00 PM onwards, while departure (Check-out) must be completed before 11:00 AM. This allows us to ensure the necessary time for deep cleaning the property.

Is it possible to check in early or check out late?

Early Check-in or Late Check-out is strictly subject to property availability (depending on whether other guests are arriving or departing on the same day). Please request this in advance through our reservations team to verify if it is feasible.

Stays & Services
What hygiene measures are implemented in the properties?

Your well-being is our top priority. We follow strict sanitization and deep cleaning protocols carried out by professional staff before every arrival, guaranteeing an immaculate and safe space.

Do the properties have high-speed Wi-Fi?

Yes, we understand that connectivity is essential. All stays in our premium collection are equipped with high-speed internet, ideal for both entertainment and meeting your remote work needs while working from home.

Are pets or events allowed in the properties?

To preserve the high standards of maintenance and design of our accommodations, parties and events are generally not permitted. Regarding pets, access depends on the specific rules of each property; we suggest reviewing the details of each listing.
